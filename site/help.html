    Public links

    Share a service with the globe button. It goes public through Tailscale Funnel on port 8443 or 10000, behind a link that expires and an optional password. Nothing else becomes public.

    Modules

    Modules stay off until you turn them on in Control › Modules. To add one, put its folder in core/modules/ on the hub that signs your updates.
